About MultiCrop Preview

MultiCrop Preview is a free tool that allows content creators, social media managers, and designers to preview their images in different aspect ratios for various social media platforms.

With our tool, you can:

  • Upload an image and see how it looks on all platforms at once
  • Visualize safe zones for important content
  • Download individual platform versions
  • Export all versions at once with batch export
  • Customize background colors for transparent images
  • Toggle grid overlays for better composition
  • Use AI-powered features for smart cropping
  • Collaborate with your team in real-time
  • Apply professional filters and effects
  • Create custom aspect ratios

All features are completely free to use!
